Here are some of the AI role-play scenarios available for each soft skill.

🤝 Salary Negotiation

You've received a job offer ₹3L below your expectation. Practice negotiating a better package while maintaining a positive relationship with the hiring manager.

⚖️ Team Conflict

Two of your team members disagree on a project approach and it's affecting deadlines. Practice mediating between them to find a collaborative solution.

🎤 Elevator Pitch

💜 Empathetic Listening

A colleague is going through a personal crisis and it's affecting their work. Practice providing emotional support while maintaining professional boundaries.

👑 Vision Communication

You're rolling out a major organizational change. Your team is resistant. Practice communicating the vision and getting buy-in from skeptical team members.
